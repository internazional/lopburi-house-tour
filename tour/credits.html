Decisions

The 0.55 m wardrobe run is shifted 2 cm west to clear the 12 cm wall. Door hinges are chosen to clear furniture. The fixed gaming-room camera cannot show the entire sofa, TV and east-wall desk together; an extra reverse still shows the desk. The 35 cm entry level change has one 17.5 cm intermediate tread. The workshop remains a separate removable collection. Unspecified details of the existing house are illustrative.
