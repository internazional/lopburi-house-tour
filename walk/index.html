Make yourself at home.

On your phone: drag the round control with your left thumb to walk. Drag anywhere on the house view with your right thumb to look around. You can do both together.

On a computer: use W A S D or the arrow keys to walk and drag the view to look. Mouse look follows your mouse; Esc releases it.

Walk through doorways and around furniture. Use “Start in a room” if you want to go straight to a particular space.

To judge size: the model uses real metres, a 1.60 m eye height and a consistent perspective. “1 m grid” adds metre squares to the floor; the room card shows the clear dimensions inside the walls.

Lighting, reflections and detailed planting are simplified for real-time movement. For the finished appearance, open the rendered photos or 360° tour.
