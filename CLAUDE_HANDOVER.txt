4. SCENE SCALE AND COMPLETED DECISIONS

The brief's plan coordinates are x east, y south, with z as height. Blender
maps these to (x, -y, z). Exported browser geometry is X east, Y up, Z south.
One Blender/web unit equals one metre. The main house occupies x 6-15,
plan y 7.8-21.6, with the attached Thai kitchen to the west.

Floor levels: main house and connector 0.00 m; garden -0.45 m; new carport
-0.35 m. Main ceilings are 2.80 m. Thai kitchen has no horizontal ceiling.

The source contains 3,776 objects and 105 packed images at this baseline;
no missing referenced textures were reported. Collections group the site,
existing house, walls, floors, roofs, room furniture, lights, and cameras.

Completed compromises to preserve rather than silently redesign:
- Wardrobe is 0.55 m deep and 2.20 m high, shifted 2 cm west to avoid the inner
  wall face: x 9.18-9.73 rather than ending at x 9.75.
- Six dining chairs are arranged three along each long side to keep access clear.
- Internal/kitchen door hinges were chosen to clear the fixed furniture.
- The required gaming camera cannot show sofa, TV, and east-wall desk together
  at the fixed angle. The required view is retained; an extra reverse still
  shows the desk. Extra bathroom/kitchen overview photographs also exist.
- The 35 cm entry difference uses an intermediate tread: two 17.5 cm risers.
- Existing mum's house is context geometry; its interior layout is unspecified
  and not furnished. Some facade details are illustrative.
- Workshop is provisional and removable. Roof/waterproofing/structural details
  that were not supplied are visual modelling decisions, not resolved designs.
- Panorama viewpoints use supplied geometric centres at 1.60 m eye height;
  living/kitchen centres lie over sofa/worktable footprints. Walking room starts
  deliberately use different clear positions so the player is not inside furniture.

Style: modern tropical Thai; warm white walls, oak/teak, black window frames,
terracotta roofs, planting, warm interior light, darker gaming room with RGB.
Assets are from Poly Haven, CC0, at 2K, downloaded/checksummed via its API.
See assets\manifest.json and output\README.txt for exact asset provenance and
the full list of self-modelled furniture, fixtures, architecture, and site items.

Original final renders used Blender 5.2.2 LTS, Cycles OPTIX, RTX 5080,
AgX and denoising: 256 maximum samples for stills/exteriors and 128 for panos.
Renderer has GPU backend detection and CPU sample reductions.
Measured final image render time was 13.21 minutes total; this excludes scene
build, downloading, QA, and saves. It is an observation on this computer.

6. CONTINUOUS WALKING VERSION — CONTINUE OR REBUILD

This version uses Three.js 0.186.1, esbuild 0.25.12, and Draco-compressed GLB.
Dependencies are pinned in the included lockfile. The public viewer is static
and hosts its own model, textures, scripts, and Draco decoder; no backend/account
is needed to view the house.

Controls: phone left-thumb joystick moves; drag the rest of the view to look.
Desktop WASD/arrows move; drag or optional mouse look changes direction.
The live plan shows position/heading; 1 m grid adds metric floor squares.
Room menu jumps to clear starts, but ordinary movement crosses doors continuously.

Navigation baseline:
  Eye height: 1.60 m above the current floor.
  Body radius: 0.18 m (36 cm diameter).
  Walking speed: 1.35 m/s.
  Maximum step up: 0.20 m; the two 17.5 cm entrance steps are traversable.
  Walls/windows/glass and furniture footprints block movement.
  Movement is subdivided to avoid passing through thin walls on a long frame.

Rooms display clear wall-face dimensions plus supplied gross plan areas.
For example, living/dining clear dimensions are 8.80 x 4.68 m; its supplied
wall-centreline plan area is 43.2 m2. Do not present those as identical measures.

The viewer limits the field of view on the viewport's longer dimension to
75 degrees to limit excessive wide-angle distortion. Perspective still affects apparent
size; rely on the metric grid/dimensions and preserved scale to judge space.

The web exporter opens the saved house.blend, changes only the export copy,
holds closed exterior doors open, and never saves back to the Blender source.
Its baseline model has 710,986 triangles, 19 mesh groups, 57 materials, 112
metric wall pieces, 151 collision boxes, 34 floor records, and 11 tree proxies.
GLB size: 6,825,912 bytes. Walls/openings are exempt from decimation.
Furniture/decor detail, trees, tile meshes, textures, lighting, and reflections
are simplified for real-time browser rendering; Cycles images remain the visual
reference for finished appearance.

Full walking rebuild, after saved scene/manifest changes:
  & $blenderExe -b -t 8 --python-exit-code 1 -P .\scripts\verify_scene.py
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\output\rebuild_walk.ps1

Inspect geometry_audit.json before running the second command. The walking
wrapper inventories the saved scene, prepares textures, exports GLB/navigation,

Do not edit the generated walk\app.js as the primary source; change app.mjs,
navigation.mjs, index.html, or style.css, then build. navigation.json and

Important dependency when editing furniture by hand:
verify_scene.py reads persisted scene properties (rooms, input_walls, openings,
furniture_registry, imported_asset_registry) into geometry_manifest.json.
Some furniture collision boxes come from that registry, not fresh mesh bounds.
Update the corresponding registry bounds when moving/resizing/adding furniture,
then regenerate and test. New geometry must have appropriate metadata/names
or exporter handling so it gets the intended wall, floor, or collision behavior.
